PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 2026-09-02
Last Updated: 2026-09-02

This Privacy Policy applies to the games developed and published by Max Cavrilon
("I," "me," or "my") as a personal, independent developer (collectively, the
"Games"). It explains my approach to your privacy. In short: my Games do not
collect, store, sell, or share any personal data about you.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. NO DATA COLLECTION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
My Games are designed to work without collecting any personal information. My
Games do not:

  - Require you to create an account or sign in.
  - Ask for your name, email address, phone number, or other contact details.
  - Collect, log, or store your IP address or location.
  - Contain third-party analytics or tracking (such as Google Analytics).
  - Contain third-party advertising or advertising identifiers.
  - Sell, rent, trade, or share any information with third parties.
  - Track you across other apps, games, or websites, or build a profile of you.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. INFORMATION I DO NOT COLLECT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Because the Games are built to run without personal data, I never collect:

  - Contact details (name, email, phone number, address).
  - Account credentials or login information.
  - Payment or financial information.
  - Precise or approximate location data.
  - Device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  - Gameplay analytics, usage patterns, or behavioral data tied to you.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
My Games may save information such as game progress, settings, and high scores
locally on your device so the Games work as expected. This data stays on your
device, is not transmitted to me or to anyone else, and is removed if you delete
the Game or clear its data.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. APP STORES AND PLATFORMS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You may download my Games through a platform or app store (such as the Apple App
Store, Google Play, or another distributor). Those platforms may collect
information under their own privacy policies when you download or purchase a
Game — for example, to process a purchase or provide aggregate download
statistics. I do not control and am not responsible for the platforms' data
practices, and I do not receive any personal data from them beyond anonymous,
aggregated figures the platform may show me. Please review the privacy policy of
the platform you use.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Because I collect no personal information from anyone, I do not knowingly collect
personal information from children of any age. My Games are safe to play without
any personal data being gathered.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. YOUR RIGHTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Privacy laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) and CCPA/CPRA (California) grant rights to
access, correct, or delete personal data. Because I hold no personal data about
you, there is nothing for me to access, correct, delete, or export. You are
protected by design.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If my practices ever change and a Game begins collecting any personal data, I
will update this Policy, revise the "Last Updated" date, and provide notice
before the change takes effect. Continued use of a Game after changes become
effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
